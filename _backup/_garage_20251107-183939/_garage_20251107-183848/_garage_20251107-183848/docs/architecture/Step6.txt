CONTEXT:
You're building Part 6 of an enterprise automotive DMS/CRM system: Complete Inventory 
Management from acquisition to sale, including trade appraisals, pricing intelligence, 
wholesale/auction tools, and marketing automation.

TECH STACK:
- Frontend: React 18 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS
- Backend: Express.js + TypeScript + Prisma ORM
- Database: PostgreSQL (multi-tenant with tenantId)
- ML Service: Python FastAPI (for price predictions)
- Parts 1-5 completed: ML, Pixel Tracking, Settings, Accounting, Infrastructure

YOUR MISSION:
Build a comprehensive inventory management system that handles the entire vehicle 
lifecycle from acquisition (trade-in, auction, wholesale) to sale, with AI-powered 
pricing, market insights, and automated reconditioning workflows.

  kbbTradeInGood    Decimal?
  kbbTradeInFair    Decimal?
