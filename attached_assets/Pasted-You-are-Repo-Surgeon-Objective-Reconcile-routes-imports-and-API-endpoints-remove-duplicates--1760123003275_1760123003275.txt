   - Apply codemods to eliminate unresolved imports and flatten obvious cycles (keep public barrels like @/components).

6) Delete/Move:
   - Remove redundant files after codemods.

7) CI Guardrails:

   - Create .github/workflows/repo-hygiene.yml that runs these on PRs and uploads repo-sweep artifacts.
